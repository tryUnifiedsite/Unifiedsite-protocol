# Unified Whitepaper

## Vision

Deep markets should not depend on luck. Unified imagines a Solana where a new project can open with a proper pool, buyers meet real depth instead of scattered fragments, and every figure on screen can be traced to a named source.

## Mission

Give teams a clear path from idea to funded pool, concentrate flow so quotes hold under size, and keep the public record honest: a fact is shown when it is confirmed and marked as waiting until then.
