What it is for

Three jobs, one surface

Easy to say, hard to do well: give a project a proper pool, stop buyers from scattering, and show each number beside its source.

I

Open a project

The plan is a guided route from rough concept to funded pool, so a team spends its hours on the product instead of the plumbing.

II

Concentrate depth

Thin pools turn each order into a gamble. The design steers orders into shared pools, so quotes stay firm when size arrives.

III

Stay legible

Each figure on this page names where it came from. A fact nobody has confirmed yet is shown as waiting, never guessed.

Path

How a project moves through

  1. Sketch the concept

    Name it, size it, decide who it is for. Nothing is deployed until the shape is settled.

  2. Seed the pool

    Pair the project with SOL and open the pool, so the earliest buyer meets a real book instead of an empty room.

  3. Let the market answer

    Prices, volume and depth show up on the same tape as everything else, open for anyone to read.
